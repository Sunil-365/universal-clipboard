Frequently Asked Questions

Is DropConnect free to use?

Yes! DropConnect offers a 100% free tier for instant clipboard and file sharing across devices. Premium features offer extended history and custom spaces.

How secure is my data?

DropConnect uses end-to-end client-side AES encryption. Your clipboard contents are encrypted before leaving your browser and decrypted only on your connected receiving device.

Which devices are supported?

DropConnect works across all major platforms including Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, iOS, and Android through any modern browser.
